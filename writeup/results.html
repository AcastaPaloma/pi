Every video is a trained model driving two simulated SO-101 arms by itself. Nothing is scripted: the cubes, the bin and the target land somewhere new each time, and the model sees only its three cameras and one sentence, such as “Hand the blue cube over to the left arm and put it in the bin.”

Two-arm models are tested on the same 30 random layouts for each of the four tasks (120 trials). Scores before October 17 were measured with an earlier renderer; the same model scores 86 there and 90 now.
