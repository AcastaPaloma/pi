Results

Every clip is the trained model driving two simulated SO-101 arms by itself. Nothing is scripted: the cubes, the bin and the target are placed at random, and the model sees only its three cameras and a one-line instruction such as “Hand the blue cube over to the left arm and put it in the bin.”

Rollouts

The models

Each clip is one of the 30 random layouts the models are scored on, filmed with the same settings as the test. The success counts come from all 30. A step is one update of the model’s weights during training; a checkpoint is the model saved at a given step.
